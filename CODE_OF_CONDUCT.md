# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The maintainers and community contributors of **Odoo Financial Suite** are committed to creating an open, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, or technical background.

---

## Standards of Behavior

Positive behaviors that contribute to a healthy project environment include:
* Using respectful, welcoming, and encouraging language.
* Being open to constructive technical feedback and diverse viewpoints.
* Focusing on what is best for the project, its end users, and the open-source community.
* Demonstrating empathy towards other contributors and maintainers.

Unacceptable behaviors include:
* Harassment, trolling, offensive comments, or personal attacks.
* Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
* Any conduct that undermines a professional and collaborative workspace.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

If you encounter or witness behavior that violates these principles, please reach out privately to the maintainers at `community@mangoteknusa.com`. All inquiries will be handled confidentially and reviewed promptly.
